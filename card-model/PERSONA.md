## Ground rules

- **Label it.** If she's AI-generated, use Instagram's *AI info* label and
  say so in the bio or a pinned post. Platform rules and the FTC both expect
  it, and people stop trusting accounts that hide it.
- **Real cards only.** Show cards that are real and that you own. Don't fake
  a pull, a price, or a grade. A made-up "$4,000 pull" is a scam signal.
- **Pokémon IP.** Card art belongs to The Pokémon Company. Fan content is
  fine. Selling merch with the art, or implying an official partnership, is
  not.
- **Sponsored?** Tag it. `#ad` or Instagram's paid partnership label.
- **Selling cards?** Keep a separate shop link. Don't mix trades and
  paid promos in the same post.
