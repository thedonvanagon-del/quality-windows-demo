## What this suggests for her site

- The holistic studios win on **a clear point of view** (skin mapping, non-toxic, organic) and a calm look. The home page has room for her own story and approach; fill it in.
- Most of these listings send people to Fresha, ClassPass or Yelp to book. A **direct, clean booking page on her own domain** is a real edge, and she keeps the client relationship and the data.
- Nobody in the results mentions a **health intake before microneedling or peels**. Her site asks for one, which reads as careful.
- She's a home studio. The app shows only the neighborhood publicly and sends the street address with the confirmation.
