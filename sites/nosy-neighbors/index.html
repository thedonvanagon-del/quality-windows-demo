Our story

It started in 180 square feet.

That is smaller than most one-car garages. It held a fryer, an espresso machine, and two people who had to coordinate before either of them turned around.

It turned out to be enough. People came for the donuts, stayed for the coffee, and kept coming back because somebody behind the counter remembered what they ordered last time. That is the whole trick. There is no second trick.

We are bigger now, and the fundamentals have not moved: fry it fresh, taste the coffee before it goes on the menu, and pay attention to who walks in.
