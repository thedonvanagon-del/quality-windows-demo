# Warm Hands, Slow Start

**Date:** Tuesday, October 27, 2026
**Length:** 30 minutes
**Theme:** Late-October mornings. Slowing down with the first cup.
**Setup:** A seat you can stay in. Phone on silent. Your coffee can wait. You'll make it after.

*Read aloud at an easy pace, or record it. Times in brackets are silence. Let them be silent.*

---

## 1. Arrive (0:00 – 3:00)

Good morning.

Find your seat. Chair, couch, floor. Doesn't matter.
Spine tall, not stiff. Hands on your knees or in your lap.
Close your eyes. Or let them go soft on a spot a few feet ahead.

[pause 20 seconds]

You have thirty minutes. Nothing to fix in them. Nothing to get right.

It's late October. The light is lower now. The air has an edge. Your body knows it.
It wants warm things. It wants slow.

Let's give it that.

Take a big breath in through your nose.
Let it out through your mouth. Make some noise if you want.

[pause 15 seconds]

Again. In.
And out.

[pause 15 seconds]

One more. This time, let the whole day's list fall out with the exhale.
The emails. The orders. The errands.
They'll still be there at eight. You can pick them back up then.

[pause 30 seconds]

Let your breathing go back to normal. Don't steer it.

[pause 35 seconds]

---

## 2. Breath (3:00 – 8:00)

Now just follow it.

Feel the air at your nostrils. Cooler on the way in. A little warmer on the way out.

[pause 30 seconds]

Count each breath out. One.
Then in. Then out. Two.
Keep going up to ten. Then start over at one.

If you lose count, that's fine. Everyone does.
Notice you drifted. Come back to one. That's the whole practice.
Noticing is the rep. You're not failing. You're training.

[pause 70 seconds]

Keep counting. Or let the counting go and just ride the breath.
Whatever feels easier today.

[pause 70 seconds]

If a thought shows up, say hello. Let it walk through.
You don't have to follow it out the door.

[pause 50 seconds]

---

## 3. Body (8:00 – 14:00)

Let your attention drop to your feet.

Feel the floor, or the ends of your socks, or the cold in your toes.
Just feel. No need to change anything.

[pause 20 seconds]

Move up to your calves. Your knees. The long muscles of your thighs.
Let them be heavy. Let the chair or the floor hold them.

[pause 30 seconds]

Your hips. Your lower back.
This is where a lot of us carry the week. See if it can set something down.

[pause 30 seconds]

Your belly. Let it be soft. You don't have to hold it in here.
Feel it rise and fall.

[pause 30 seconds]

Your chest. Your ribs.
Something steady is going on in there. It has been since before you were born.
You didn't have to do anything for it.

[pause 30 seconds]

Your shoulders. Let them drop an inch. Then maybe another.

Your arms. Your elbows. Your wrists.
Your hands. Open them a little. Feel the air on your palms.

[pause 35 seconds]

Your neck. Your jaw. Unclench it. Let your tongue go soft.
Your eyes. Your forehead. The small muscles around your eyes.
The top of your head.

[pause 50 seconds]

Now feel all of it at once. One body, sitting here, breathing.

[pause 50 seconds]

---

## 4. The Cup (14:00 – 22:00)

Now picture your kitchen. Early.

The kettle is on. You can hear it ticking as it heats, then the low rumble before the boil.

[pause 20 seconds]

You open the bag of beans. Lean in.
That first smell. Roasted, a little sweet. Maybe cocoa. Maybe toasted nuts. Maybe something like dried fruit.
It's the smell of a good morning that hasn't happened yet.

[pause 30 seconds]

You grind them. The sound is loud, then it stops.
The room smells like coffee now. Fuller. Warmer.

[pause 20 seconds]

You pour the water. The grounds puff up and bubble. That's the bloom.
Gas is leaving the coffee. It's fresh. It's alive.
You wait. You don't rush it.

[pause 30 seconds]

Now the cup is in your hands.
Feel the warmth move into your palms. Up your wrists. Into your chest.

Bring it close. Breathe in the steam.

[pause 20 seconds]

First sip. Slow.
Notice the first thing. Bright, or round, or bitter, or sweet.
Notice what's left on your tongue after you swallow.

[pause 35 seconds]

Now go back.

This coffee started as a seed, planted by someone, on a hillside somewhere.
It grew slow, high up where the air is thin and cool.
That slow growth is where the flavor comes from.

[pause 20 seconds]

Someone picked the cherries by hand. Someone sorted them.
Someone laid them out in the sun and turned them, day after day.
Someone bagged them. Someone shipped them. Someone roasted them with careful timing.

[pause 30 seconds]

Every cup is a lot of people's mornings before yours.

You don't have to make that into something big. Just let it be true for a moment.
You're part of a long line of hands.

[pause 50 seconds]

Let the picture fade. Come back to the breath.
Warm hands. Slow start.

[pause 70 seconds]

---

## 5. Intention (22:00 – 27:00)

Stay with the breath. Now we'll set up the day. Three quiet questions.
Don't answer with words. Let whatever comes, come.

First. What do I want to bring into today?
Patience. Focus. Humor. Something else.
Let one thing rise.

[pause 50 seconds]

Second. What can I leave alone today?
Something that isn't mine to carry. A worry that has no action attached.
Let it sit on the table. You can walk past it.

[pause 50 seconds]

Third. What's one thing I'll actually taste, or hear, or feel today?
One moment I won't rush through. A meal. A walk. A conversation. A cup.
Pick it now. Promise it to yourself.

[pause 50 seconds]

Now boil all of it down to one word.
Just one. Say it silently, once.

[pause 30 seconds]

Say it again. Let it settle in your chest.

[pause 30 seconds]

---

## 6. Return (27:00 – 30:00)

Start to widen your attention.

Hear the room. Whatever sounds are there. Near ones, far ones.

[pause 20 seconds]

Feel your weight in the seat. Feel your hands.

[pause 20 seconds]

Take a deeper breath in. Let it out slow.

[pause 15 seconds]

Wiggle your fingers. Wiggle your toes.
Roll your shoulders. Stretch your neck, side to side.

[pause 20 seconds]

When you're ready, open your eyes.
Take a second before you stand. Let the room come back.

[pause 30 seconds]

That's thirty minutes. Nicely done.

Now go make your coffee. Grind it fresh. Watch the bloom.
Drink the first few sips without your phone.

Have a good day.

---

## Notes for the cup

Today's pairing is a medium roast with cocoa and toasted-nut notes, brewed as a pour-over.
The slow pour matches the mood. If you're short on time, a French press works. Same idea: don't rush the first sip.
