# Warm Hands, Slow Start

**Date:** Tuesday, October 27, 2026
**Length:** about 90 seconds
**Theme:** Late-October mornings. One slow sip.
**Format:** Vertical video for Instagram Reels. Voiceover over a slow shot of a mug, steam, or pour-over.

*Read at an easy pace. Times in brackets are silence. Let them be silent.*

---

## Script

**0:00 – 0:08**

Before you pick up your phone, give me ninety seconds.

[pause 2 seconds]

**0:08 – 0:30**

Sit up. Wrap your hands around your mug. Or rest them on your knees.
Close your eyes.

Breathe in through your nose.
Let it out slow.

[pause 4 seconds]

Again. In.
And out.

[pause 4 seconds]

**0:30 – 0:55**

Feel your shoulders drop.

It's late October. The air has an edge now.
Your body wants something warm.
Let it have that.

[pause 4 seconds]

**0:55 – 1:15**

Picture the cup. The steam coming off it.
Smell it. Cocoa. Toasted nuts. Something a little sweet.

Feel the heat move into your palms.
That's all you need to do right now.
Be here for one sip.

[pause 5 seconds]

**1:15 – 1:30**

Pick one word for today.
Say it to yourself, once.

[pause 5 seconds]

Open your eyes.
Take the first sip slow.

Good morning.
