# The Middle Way Pour

**Date:** Friday, November 13, 2026
**Length:** about 60 seconds
**Theme:** Buddhist basics for a Friday. The middle way, impermanence, beginner's mind. Told through coffee.
**Format:** Vertical video for Instagram Reels. Voiceover over a slow pour-over, or steam rising off a mug.

*Read at an easy pace. Times in brackets are silence. Let them be silent.*

---

## Script

**0:00 – 0:06**

Friday morning. Sixty seconds. Grab your mug.

[pause 2 seconds]

**0:06 – 0:22**

Sit up. Close your eyes.
Breathe in.
And out. Slow.

[pause 4 seconds]

Again. In.
And out.

[pause 4 seconds]

**0:22 – 0:36**

The Buddha taught the middle way.
Not too tight. Not too loose.

Same as a pour-over.
Rush it, and it's sour.
Stew it, and it's bitter.
Slow and steady hits the sweet spot.

[pause 3 seconds]

**0:36 – 0:48**

Nothing lasts.
Not the steam. Not the week. Not even the crema.

So drink the cup while it's here.

[pause 4 seconds]

**0:48 – 1:00**

Beginner's mind.
Every cup is your first cup.

Take one sip like you've never tasted coffee.

[pause 3 seconds]

Happy Friday.
