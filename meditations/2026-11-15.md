# Slow Drip Sunday

**Date:** Sunday, November 15, 2026
**Length:** about 75 seconds
**Theme:** Rest. Do one thing at a time. A Zen teaching, told through coffee.
**Format:** Vertical video for Instagram Reels. Calm voiceover over a still sunrise beach, with soft wave sound.

The narration lines and the pause after each one are in `2026-11-15.script.json`. The caption timings are in `2026-11-15.captions.json`.

## Script

Sunday morning. Nowhere to be. Just you and your cup.

Sit down. Let your shoulders drop.

Breathe in.
And let it go.

Once more. In.
And let it go.

There's an old Zen teaching.
When you walk, just walk. When you sit, just sit.
So when you drink your coffee, just drink your coffee.

No phone. No list. No Monday.

Today is a slow drip. Not a double shot.

Feel the warm cup in your hands.
Smell it before you sip.

This is the whole practice. Being here for something small.

The week can wait. It will still be there after the last sip.

Enjoy your Sunday.
