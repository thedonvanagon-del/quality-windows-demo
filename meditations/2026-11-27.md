## Script

Friday morning. The day after Thanksgiving. You are mostly pie at this point.

Sit down. Slowly. Hold your mug with both hands.

Breathe in.
And let it go.

Again. In.
And out.

The Buddha called contentment the greatest wealth.
That's good news. You already have coffee. And leftovers.

Everyone else is fighting over a parking spot right now.
You are fighting nothing. You have a warm cup and stretchy pants.

Think of one thing you're thankful for. Besides the coffee.
Hold it for one slow breath.

Now open your eyes. Take a sip.

Happy Friday.
