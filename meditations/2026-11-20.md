# Empty Your Cup

**Date:** Friday, November 20, 2026
**Length:** about 80 seconds
**Theme:** The end of the week. Let it go. The Zen "empty your cup" story, with coffee jokes.
**Format:** Vertical video for Instagram Reels. Calm voiceover (voice 3, Nicole) over a still, blurred coast, with soft wave sound.

The narration lines and the pause after each one are in `2026-11-20.script.json`. The caption timings are in `2026-11-20.captions.json`.

## Script

Friday morning. You made it. The week is almost out of beans.

Sit down. Put your shoulders back where they belong.

Breathe in.
And let it go.

One more. In.
And out.

There's an old Zen story. A full cup can't take in anything new.
So empty yours. The meetings. The emails. That one thing you said on Tuesday.

Pour it all out.

Whatever didn't get done this week has gone cold. Don't reheat it.

Leave room for the weekend. And for a refill.

Open your eyes. Take a slow sip.

Happy Friday.
