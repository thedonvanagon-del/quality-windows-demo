## Script

Monday morning. Deep breath. You've got this. And you've got coffee.

Sit up tall. Hold your mug like it's the last one on earth.

Breathe in.
And let it go.

Again. In.
And out.

The Buddha taught that life has suffering. He never saw your inbox.
He also never had a fresh pour-over. So you're one up.

Nothing is an emergency until the second cup.

Take the week one sip at a time. Not the whole pot at once.

You are not behind. You are still brewing.

Pick one thing to do well today. Just one.

Now open your eyes. Take a sip.

Go get your Monday.
